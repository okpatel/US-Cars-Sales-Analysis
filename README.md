# 🚗 US Car Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview
The **US Car Sales Analysis** project is a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to provide dynamic and interactive visual insights into car sales data. It enables stakeholders to evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), track trends, and make data-driven decisions to optimize sales strategies.

## 🎯 Objective
To create a user-friendly and interactive platform using Power BI that empowers users to:
- Monitor sales performance with real-time metrics.
- Extract actionable insights from KPIs.
- Analyze car sales trends across various dimensions.

## 📁 File Included
- `US Cars Sales Analysis.pbix`: Power BI Dashboard file

## 🔧 Tools Used
- **Excel** – Data Cleaning & Preprocessing  
- **Power BI** – Data Modeling, Visualization & Dashboard Design

## 📌 KPIs Tracked
### Sales Overview
- Year-to-Date (YTD) Total Sales  
- Month-to-Date (MTD) Total Sales  
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth in Sales  
- YTD vs Previous Year-to-Date (PYTD) Sales  

### Average Price Analysis
- YTD & MTD Average Price  
- YoY Growth in Average Price  
- Difference vs PYTD  

### Cars Sold Metrics
- YTD & MTD Units Sold  
- YoY Growth in Cars Sold  
- Difference vs PYTD  

## 📈 Visuals & Charts
- **YTD Sales Weekly Trend** – Line Chart  
- **Sales by Body Style** – Pie Chart  
- **Sales by Color** – Pie Chart  
- **Cars Sold by Dealer Region** – Bar Chart  
- **Company-Wise Sales Trend** – Table  
- **Detailed Car Sales Grid** – Full data table

## 🛠 Data Preparation Steps

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing on top-selling colors (e.g., Pearl White) and body styles (SUVs).  
- Analyze underperforming regions and apply successful strategies from high-performing ones.

- Benchmark sales strategies against top competitors.

This Power BI dashboard successfully delivers a central platform for analyzing US car sales. With rich visuals and critical KPIs, it empowers stakeholders to make data-informed decisions and fine-tune sales strategies for improved business outcomes.
